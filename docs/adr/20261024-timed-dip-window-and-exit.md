# Timed dip window and time exit

Accepted 2026-10-23 with the approved [timed dip window and exit plan](../plans/archive/20261023-timed-dip-window-and-exit.md), with the recommendation of each of its four open questions; decision 6 amended after review on 2026-10-24 (the plan's Option B, the 60 s sale limit). Engine `dip-paper-v4`. It amends the [dip-entry decision](20261008-dip-entry-strategy.md), the [bonding-curve execution decision](20261009-bonding-curve-execution.md), [outcome-bounded market capture](20261015-outcome-bounded-market-capture.md), the [agreed-strategy preset](20261016-agreed-strategy-run-preset.md) and the [market window lead](20261022-market-window-lead.md).

## Context

Most new Pump.fun tokens live about 3 minutes, so the strategy has to act in seconds and get out before the token dies. `dip-entry` v1 under engine `dip-paper-v3` had one clock: `horizonSlots` (4,500 slots, about 16 minutes at the measured pace) from the tracked wallet's buy.

- **The dip watch** triggered on any trade at or below the dip level until the horizon end. In run `a5f5…2674` the dip came about 12 minutes after the buy.
- **A position** ended only on take profit or stop loss. Without either it stayed `OPEN` as `HORIZON_REACHED`, and a trigger with no later trade stayed `OPEN` as `NO_EXIT_FILL`: a token that stopped trading was never sold.
- **A `DECIDED` market capture** was cut by `SimulationConfig.mvp()`, which decides most untriggered or open cycles only at the horizon end, so it saved little.

The agreed `decu-dip` v2 spec ([docs/strategy/decu-dip-v2-spec.md](../strategy/decu-dip-v2-spec.md)) sets the clocks:

- **P7, Section 6:** our buy is submitted at most 30.000 s after `t0`, the block time of the wallet's buy; exactly 30 s counts.
- **P11, Section 8:** `exit_start = min(entry_confirmed + 3 min, t0 + 4 min)`. Take profit, stop loss and time are triggers and the first wins. The position is sold 100%; `UNRESOLVED` is only for a sale that cannot be made.
- **Section 11:** the market is recorded for 4 minutes from `t0` (`recordingWindowMs: 240000`), even when the signal is skipped.

On the bonding curve the curve itself is the counterparty: a sale never needs another trader, and the curve at any moment is the post-trade curve of the last trade before it.

## Decision

1. **Clocks come from block times, never slot counts.** `t0` is the wallet buy's `blockTime`, a trade's time its `observedTime`, the fill's time its fill trade's `observedTime`, and the recording's end the coverage end time recorded at capture. All are whole-second `Instant`s from the dataset, so a run stays deterministic and reads no clock. Slot still orders everything. This narrows trading-domain's "wall-clock time is metadata": block time is on-chain data the dataset carries and checksums, not the time a run happens.
2. **The clocks are optional.** `SimulationConfig` gains `dipWindowMs` (1,000–600,000) and the pair `maxHoldMs` / `hardExitStartMs` (1,000–3,600,000 each), set together. With none set, outcomes equal `dip-paper-v3`'s except rule 6 below. The configuration hash names a clock only when it is set, so `mvp()`'s hash is unchanged. Each intent stores `t0` (`trade_intents.t0`).
3. **The dip window is inclusive and gates the trigger, not the fill.** A trade can trigger only if `observedTime ≤ t0 + dipWindowMs`; the watch stops at the first trade later than that, and no trigger is `NO_DIP`, decided at that trade's slot (or the horizon end without one). A timed configuration on a buy without a block time is `NO_DIP` at the buy's slot.
4. **The exit starts at `exitStart = min(fillTime + maxHoldMs, t0 + hardExitStartMs)`, and time wins from then on.** Take profit and stop loss are checked only on trades with `observedTime < exitStart`; a trade at or after it, in the same whole second included, lands after the exit started and never changes the reason. The first trigger fixes the reason. Each position stores `exitStart` and each trigger its block `time` (a time exit's is `exitStart`).
5. **A time exit sells against the curve standing at `exitStart`:** the post-trade curve of the last eligible trade before it after the fill, counting the trades later in the fill's own slot (in canonical order), or the fill trade's own; a curve completing later in that slot is a migration. The whole position sells through `BondingCurve.sell` with the same fees, rounding and `transactionCostSol` as any exit, and the close is flagged `priceAssumption = STANDING_CURVE`. It is valid only when the recording proves it covers `exitStart`: a recorded trade of the pair at or after it, or a coverage end time at or after it. With complete coverage that curve is the market's real state, not a stale or interpolated price, so the "no silent price fallback" rule holds. Without the proof the position stays `OPEN` as `HORIZON_REACHED`. The trigger's slot, and the decision slot, is the first slot the recording proves at or after `exitStart`.
6. **A take profit or stop loss sells into the next trade only when it lands within 60 s of the trigger; otherwise it sells against the curve standing at its sale slot.** The 60 s are spec Section 8's active phase (`exitRetry.activePhaseMs`), an engine rule of `dip-paper-v4` for every run, timed or not, kept out of the configuration hash. The deadline is `trigger.time + 60 s`, inclusive in whole seconds.
   - `NEXT_TRADE`: the first eligible trade at or after `saleSlot = trigger.slot + latencySlots` whose block time is at most the deadline and whose curve can pay. Trades within the deadline whose curve cannot pay are skipped; when some were tried and none paid, the position is `UNRESOLVED`.
   - `STANDING_CURVE`: with no such trade, against the post-trade curve of the last eligible trade before `saleSlot` (normally the trigger). Valid only when the recording proves it reaches `saleSlot` and that no unseen trade can land within the deadline: a recorded trade of the pair, eligible or not, at or after `saleSlot` and later than the deadline, or a coverage end time strictly later than it (an unseen later trade is at or after that time). Without the proof the position stays `OPEN` as `HORIZON_REACHED`; datasets of schema v3 and v4 have no coverage end time, so only a later trade proves it.
   - Both the standing-curve sale and its `UNRESOLVED` are decided at the slot proving the deadline, the first recorded trade past it or the coverage end slot, so a `DECIDED` capture cut there reports the outcome a full capture would. The close keeps the sale slot as its own slot, and the console dates it at the trigger's time.

   This replaces `NO_EXIT_FILL` in every run (plan Open Question 4).
7. **`UNRESOLVED` means no sale is possible:** the curve to sell against cannot pay the sale. The position stays `OPEN`. A completed curve before the sale is still `MIGRATED`. `NO_EXIT_FILL` stays readable for stored runs and is never produced by `dip-paper-v4`.
8. **The coverage end time is recorded at capture.** When a window's coverage completes, the market capture reads the block time of the nearest produced block at or below its last slot once (`getBlockTime`, stepping back with `getBlocks` over a skipped slot), counted as API usage purpose `BLOCK_TIME`, and stores it as `throughBlockTime` in market manifest version 5. The export writes it as dataset schema v6 `coverage[].throughTime`. The nearest earlier block's time is never later than the slot's true time, so the proof is conservative. A failed read leaves it null, logged `WARN`, and is retried on resume. While a `DECIDED` decision waits on time, the cutoff also reads the block time of the captured prefix's end, at most once per 100-transaction batch (plus `getBlocks` over a skipped slot). Manifest versions 2 to 4 never read block times.
9. **The spec's configuration is a strategy-owned value, `SimulationConfig.agreed()`:** 0.1 SOL, dip 2,000 bps within 30 s, take profit 5,500, stop loss 4,000, minimum $10,000, latency 1, 0.001 SOL, a 3-minute hold and a 4-minute hard exit start, and 4,500 slots as the recording bound. The console preset is built from it, and the `DECIDED` cutoff and wallet + token qualification switch from `mvp()` to it, so both judge a capture with the same configuration. `mvp()` stays the API and CLI default.
10. **A `DECIDED` window stops when its agreed outcome is decided within the captured prefix and the prefix's coverage end time reaches `t0 + 240 s`.** Every token keeps at least its first 4 minutes, even one skipped at the buy, so charts and reruns with other dip or market cap values still work inside them; a position whose exit starts at 4 minutes is decided once its sale is proven, so a window typically stops about 4–5 minutes after the buy. A skip such as `NO_DIP` is decided at the first trade after the 30 s window but still recorded through 4 minutes. The minimum is `ReplayMarketCaptureRequest.MINIMUM_DECIDED_RECORDING`. The cutoff never decides inside the lead. A failed or missing block time leaves the window undecided and it is captured in full. The `CUTOFF_DECIDED` recommendation states the agreed levels, clocks and minimum, and its caveat is `CUTOFF_USES_AGREED_STRATEGY`; the legacy-comparison recommendation carries `COMPARISON_MAY_NEED_FULL_CAPTURE`.

## Alternatives

- **Slot-count clocks** (for example 30 s as about 140 slots): the slot pace drifts, and the spec's clocks are times. Block times are on-chain and already in the dataset.
- **Selling a time exit into the next trade after `exitStart`:** a dying token has no next trade, which is the case the time exit exists for, and a moment in time has no slot the data gives to sell at. The standing curve can be optimistic by at most about one slot's trades landing before our sale; the spec accepts ±1 s (plan Open Question 1).
- **Keeping `NO_EXIT_FILL`:** it leaves a sellable position unsold.
- **Selling a take profit or stop loss into the next trade however late** (the first version of decision 6): the close then depended on trades up to the horizon end while it was decided at the sale slot, so a `DECIDED` capture cut after the sale slot could report a different P&L than a full capture. Selling 20 minutes after a trigger into whatever trade came is also not what a bot with a 60 s active phase does.
- **Cutting `DECIDED` captures with `mvp()`:** qualification would then judge a capture with other levels than the one that cut it, and refuse every cut capture for coverage.
- **Backfilling block times for existing captures:** RPC calls against old captures. Re-capture the tokens worth studying instead (plan Open Question 3).

## Consequences

- Every run id changes once with the engine version; `mvp()`'s configuration hash `be93…a329` does not. Untimed outcomes are unchanged except the former `NO_EXIT_FILL` cycles, which close against the standing curve once the recording proves no trade landed within 60 s, and a take profit or stop loss whose next trade came more than 60 s after its trigger, which now sells against the standing curve instead of into that trade. The bundled v3 sample cannot prove its former `NO_EXIT_FILL` cycle's 60 s, so that position stays `OPEN` as `HORIZON_REACHED`: the sample closes 2 of 10 cycles for `-0.013487824000` SOL. `DECIDED` cutoffs recorded under `dip-paper-v3` reopen on resume, since their identity (engine version and configuration hash) changes.
- Captures cut by the agreed clocks hold about 4–5 minutes per token, so a run with longer settings on them (for example a 15-minute hold) is refused `MARKET_COVERAGE_ENDED`. `FULL` mode still records the whole 4,500 slots for longer experiments (plan Open Question 2).
- Datasets of schema v3 to v5 and captures before manifest version 5 have no coverage end time: a time exit on them is proven only by a later trade, so on a token that stopped trading the position stays `OPEN` as `HORIZON_REACHED`.
- New nullable columns `trade_intents.t0`, `positions.exit_start`, `positions.exit_trigger_time` and `positions.close_price_assumption`, and the enum values `TIME_EXIT` and `UNRESOLVED`. Production applies [V20261024120000__timed_exit.sql](../schema/V20261024120000__timed_exit.sql) by hand; a local database Hibernate created needs it for its enum checks. Stored runs still read: a close without a price assumption reads as `NEXT_TRADE`, and a trigger without a time is dated by its trade.
- The preset's unsupported rules drop from 8 to 6: `DIP_WINDOW_30_SECONDS` and `TIMED_EXIT` are applied, and Section 8's remaining gap is `EXIT_RETRY_ECONOMICS`.
- Section 8's retry economics are not modelled: a paper sale lands at its first attempt and pays one `transactionCostSol`, with no slippage steps, priority fee escalation, fee cap or failed attempts.

## Revisit when

- Exit retries and their costs are modelled (`EXIT_RETRY_ECONOMICS`);
- sub-second timing or within-slot order is recorded;
- the pool after migration is priced, so a migration while holding can be sold;
- the `decu-dip` v2 engine ships and takes over the spec's clocks.
